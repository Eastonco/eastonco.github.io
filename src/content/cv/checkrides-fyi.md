# checkrides.fyi

## Overview

checkrides.fyi is a platform I built connecting pilots with Designated Pilot Examiners (DPEs) — essentially a Yelp-style review and discovery tool for exam providers. The idea came out of a gap I ran into myself while navigating my own checkrides: there wasn't a good way to find or evaluate DPEs based on other pilots' experiences.

## Build & Stack

The site was entirely vibe-coded using Claude, and is hosted on Vercel with Supabase as the backend. I'm tracking usage and engagement through PostHog analytics.

## Traction

It's already picked up a handful of early users. It's a fun project that lets me combine my aviation experience with my computer science and engineering background.
