# Videography & Photography Experience

Videography was a huge part of my life for a long time, spanning from high school through college.

## Sports Photography
**High School**

I got my start in high school making sports highlight videos for basketball and football, primarily working in photography at this stage.

## Director of Videography, Zzu Cru
**Washington State University**

In college, I shifted from photography into videography and became Director of Videography for Zzu Cru. I created videos for fans to drive engagement, had press passes granting me field access, and produced sports promo videos across football, basketball, soccer, volleyball, and more.

## Videographer, Student Entertainment Board
**Washington State University**

I also worked as a videographer for the Student Entertainment Board, where I made concert highlight recap videos for artists including T-Pain, Jack Harlow, Tai Verdes, Audien, and AC Slater.

## Freelance Videographer — Greek Life
**Washington State University**

Alongside my campus roles, I contracted out my videography services to Greek Life, creating bid day videos and recruitment videos for sororities, including Alpha Delta Pi.

## Portfolio

All of my videos can be found on YouTube at [youtube.com/@Eastonco](https://www.youtube.com/@Eastonco).
