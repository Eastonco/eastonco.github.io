# Overview

I'm a Software Engineer III at Expedia Group, where I've grown from a frontend-focused intern into a full-stack engineer leading AI infrastructure adoption across one of the company's core platforms. My day-to-day spans React, GraphQL, TypeScript, Kotlin, and Spring Boot, but the thread that ties most of my work together — at Expedia and outside of it — is finding ways to make complex systems more usable, whether that's for 500+ internal admins, 800+ coworkers learning a new AI tool, or a handful of pilots trying to find a good DPE.

Teaching has been a constant alongside engineering. I started as a TA in college, spent a year teaching high schoolers through Microsoft TEALS, guest lecture at WSU and North Seattle College, and have trained hundreds of colleagues on AI tooling internally at Expedia. I also founded and run Expedia's internal Finance Club, and mentor interns and early-career engineers as part of my day-to-day role.

Outside of work, I'm a private pilot working toward my instrument rating, flying Cessna 172s and 182s. That interest led me to build checkrides.fyi, a platform connecting pilots with examiners — one of several side projects where I use AI to turn a fun, sometimes silly idea (like a receipt printer anyone on the internet can print to) into something real. I think that kind of build-for-fun-and-see-what-happens mindset is one of the most underrated parts of being an engineer, and it's a big part of why I still love the craft.
