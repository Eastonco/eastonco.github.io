# Aviation Experience

## Background

Aviation has been part of my life since childhood — my father flies for Alaska Airlines, so I grew up deeply immersed in it. I started pursuing my own path in aviation during my senior year of college in 2021, beginning with the FAA medical certification process. Due to a history of epilepsy, this turned into a long gauntlet of testing — MRIs, EEGs, and everything in between — that stretched into a two-to-three-year process. For a long stretch of it, I genuinely wasn't sure I'd be cleared to fly. Getting through it and finally starting flight training made earning my certificate feel like an even bigger milestone.

## Private Pilot Certificate
**Rainier Flight Service, Seattle | Checkride passed November 2024**

I trained out of Rainier Flight Service in Seattle and earned my Private Pilot certificate in just three months and around 60 hours of flight training, passing my checkride in November 2024. Since then, I've continued flying regularly and earned a high-performance endorsement, flying both Cessna 172s and 182s, all equipped with Garmin G1000 glass cockpits.

## Instrument Rating (In Progress)
**Checkride Scheduled July 20, 2026**

I've spent the past several months, since around November 2025, working toward my instrument rating and have a checkride scheduled for July 20, 2026. I don't plan to pursue aviation as a career — it's something I do purely because I love it, and instrument training has been a natural next step in building on that.
